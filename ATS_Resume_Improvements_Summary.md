## 🎨 Formatting for ATS Success

### What Makes This ATS-Friendly:

1. **Clean Structure** - No complex tables, text boxes, or columns
2. **Standard Fonts** - Uses Calibri/Arial equivalents
3. **Proper Headings** - Clear section titles in ALL CAPS or Bold
4. **Keywords Placement** - Technical skills appear early and throughout
5. **White Space** - Adequate spacing for readability
6. **Bullet Points** - Standard bullets, not custom symbols
7. **No Headers/Footers** - All content in main document body
8. **Linear Format** - Top-to-bottom reading flow
9. **Standard File Format** - .docx is universally compatible

### ATS Scanning Priority:
1. ✅ Name & Contact (Top)
2. ✅ Professional Summary (Lines 1-7)
3. ✅ **Technical Skills (Lines 8-30)** ← NOW PROMINENT
4. ✅ Experience with Keywords (Lines 31-80)
5. ✅ Education & Certifications (Lines 81-100)
